Honest limitations

• No liveness detection. A printed photo or a phone screen showing a colleague's face will pass. This deters casual proxy attendance; it does not prevent deliberate fraud.

• Accuracy degrades with poor lighting, masks, glasses changes, large weight/hair changes, and for faces underrepresented in the training data. Always keep the manual fallback available.

• Biometric data is regulated (India DPDP Act, GDPR Art. 9, Illinois BIPA). Written consent, a stated retention period, and deletion on exit are legal requirements in most jurisdictions — not optional extras.

• Single-browser storage. This is not a multi-site HR system. For that you need a server, a database, and audit logging.

• A stamped photo is evidence, not proof. The stamp is drawn by this page from the device's own clock and GPS. Both can be spoofed on a rooted/jailbroken or developer-mode device. It raises the effort required to cheat; it does not make the record tamper-proof. Server-side timestamping is the only real fix.

• GPS indoors is unreliable. Accuracy of 20–500 m is normal inside a building, and browsers often fall back to Wi-Fi/IP location, which can be kilometres off. Do not use the coordinates to enforce a tight geofence.
